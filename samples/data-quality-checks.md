# Prop-Firm Data Quality Checklist

## Purpose
A repeatable checklist for reviewing prop-firm plan information before it is published or updated.

## Validation Checks

- [ ] Confirm the firm and plan names against an official source.
- [ ] Verify account size, evaluation fees, and profit targets.
- [ ] Check maximum drawdown and daily loss limit rules.
- [ ] Review consistency requirements and minimum trading days.
- [ ] Verify payout eligibility, restrictions, and payout conditions.
- [ ] Confirm whether drawdown is static, trailing, or end-of-day.
- [ ] Record the official source URL for each important rule.
- [ ] Record the date the information was last verified.
- [ ] Flag conflicting or unclear information for further review.
- [ ] Review all changes before publishing.

## Quality Principles

1. Prefer official firm websites and rule documents over third-party summaries.
2. Never guess when a rule is unclear.
3. Keep fictional examples separate from verified real-world data.
4. Document meaningful changes so they can be reviewed later.
5. Recheck time-sensitive information such as promotions and fees.

## Example Status

This checklist is a demonstration of a proposed review workflow. It does not represent a completed audit of any specific prop firm.
